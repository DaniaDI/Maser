Frequently Asked Questions

What technologies do you use for web development?

We use modern, industry-standard technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Node.js, and various cloud services to ensure your project is fast, scalable, and secure.

How long does it take to complete a project?

The timeline depends on the complexity and scope of the project. A simple landing page might take 1-2 weeks, while a full web application could take several months.

Do you provide ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes, we offer various maintenance and support packages to ensure your website or application remains up-to-date, secure, and running smoothly after launch.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We specialize in modernizing legacy websites, improving their UI/UX, performance, and mobile responsiveness while preserving your core brand identity.
